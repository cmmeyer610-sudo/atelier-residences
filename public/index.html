A GUIDE TO THE COLLECTION

A different perspective.

Move around

Drag with the left mouse button to orbit. Scroll to zoom. Right-drag to pan. On a touch screen, drag with one finger to orbit; pinch or use two fingers to zoom and pan.

Start with a scene

Choose an exterior, room, plan, or section. Each supplied view restores its saved camera and available cut planes and layer visibility. You can keep orbiting after selecting it.

Make it your view

Use White model or Wireframe to inspect the form. Toggle the roof, furniture, site, or framing. Press R to reset the saved view and F to fit the building.

Keep the originals

Download the complete browser GLB and saved scenes. Live 3D has camera-local authored lights, sky reflections, contact shading and soft shadows. Rendered view shows the best available original offline still for each camera; it does not respond to live finish or lighting controls.

Use Plan navigator to choose a room, Architectural camera to correct verticals and frame exports, and Walkthrough director to author a timed route. Double-click a visible surface to focus. Walk uses WASD or arrows and drag-to-look; Esc returns to orbit. Star scenes, save camera views, compare matching daylight/evening studies and use Workspace for exports, units and theme. R resets a scene; F fits the residence; arrows step through scenes in orbit mode. Use Ctrl/⌘ + K to search commands and all saved perspectives. The ViewCube follows model axes. In Present, Photo mode traces the current visible model and exports native 1080p or 4K PNG stills. Photo mode needs WebGL 2 and a floating-point render target; it supports uncut material views. It uses its own lighting calculation and does not reproduce Enscape images. Offline images are labeled with their original renderer.
